# Audit

A chronological **event log** of everything the harness does: requests, agent thoughts and
tool calls, version commits, reboots, rollbacks, and errors.

## Use it to

- **Follow a self-mod** step by step — what the agent did and why a version shipped (or
  rolled back).
- **Debug** — engine errors, failed boots, and interruptions are all recorded with a tone
  and icon so problems stand out.
- **Trace history** — a durable record independent of the chat UI.

It's read-only — a faithful account of what happened, not something you edit.
