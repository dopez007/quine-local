# Instructions

This tab is your **manual** for the app — one short guide per tab and feature, explaining
what it does and how to use it. Browse the list on the left and read whichever you need.

## Make it your own

- **Edit** any doc to add your own notes or rephrase it for your team.
- **New** adds a doc — handy for documenting your own conventions or a workflow you've set up.
- **Reset** returns a doc you've edited back to the version it shipped with.

Your edits are saved and kept across updates, so the manual reflects how *you* use the app.

## It stays current automatically

As the app gains new features, the matching guide appears here too — so this manual keeps
up with the app without you having to maintain it yourself.
